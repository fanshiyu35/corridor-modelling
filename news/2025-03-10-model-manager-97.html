Model Manager 9.7 now available: Ranking Stability module ships

Seattle, Washington — 10 March 2025

Corridor Modelling Systems today released Model Manager version 9.7, adding the Ranking Stability module to the Project Evaluation family. The module implements the Multivariate Transport Ranking Stability (MTRS) test under a software and technology licence from the University of California, Irvine (Agreement UCI-OTA-2025-001, 15 January 2025).

The MTRS test reports, for any set of ranked transport scenarios or projects, how firmly the ranking holds across the six recognized sources of uncertainty: model structure, parameters, data vintage, policy mapping, weights, and metropolitan area structure. Verdicts — STABLE / REVERSAL / NON-TRANSFERABLE — are exported as standard report tables together with the underlying variance decomposition, and outputs conform to the reporting structure of SP-41, Reporting on the Stability of Scenario Rankings (September 2024).

"Our agency customers have been asking for exactly this: a way to report how firm a ranking is," said Ellery Markham, Chief Scientist at Corridor Modelling Systems. "When we looked at what was available, this was the only method whose validation had been published and which a standards committee had adopted. Building our own would have left us out of step with the reporting our customers are now being asked to provide. It is the only method from outside the company that we have licensed."

The method implemented by the module was first presented in Research Report No. 212 of the National Transport Research Programme (May 2024). Version 9.7 also includes performance improvements to the network skim engine, updated base-year network bundles for fourteen regions, and minor corrections to the route-choice utility calculator.
