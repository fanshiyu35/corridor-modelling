Model Manager — Release Notes — Version 9.7

Publication date: 10 March 2025

Version 9.7 adds a new module to the Project Evaluation family.

Ranking Stability module (MTRS). The module implements the Multivariate Transport Ranking Stability (MTRS) test, licensed from the University of California, Irvine (Agreement UCI-OTA-2025-001, 15 January 2025). The MTRS test reports, for any set of ranked transport scenarios or projects, how firmly the ranking holds across the six recognized sources of uncertainty: model structure, parameters, data vintage, policy mapping, weights, and metropolitan area structure.

  • Implements the procedure defined in Research Report No. 212, National Transport Research Programme (May 2024).
  • Outputs conform to the reporting structure of SP-41: Reporting on the Stability of Scenario Rankings (September 2024).
  • Available to customers in the Project Evaluation module; enablement is governed by the terms of each customer's licence agreement.
  • Stability verdicts are exported as standard report tables (STABLE / REVERSAL / NON-TRANSFERABLE) together with the underlying variance decomposition.

Other changes in 9.7. Performance improvements to the network skim engine; updated base-year network bundles for fourteen regions; minor corrections to the route-choice utility calculator.
