Where the module is in use — public-sector case summaries

Customer names are withheld under our customer-confidentiality commitments. The summaries below describe the kinds of public-sector organisations using the Ranking Stability module in production.

State department of transportation

Long-range plan scenario rankings, western United States

A state department of transportation runs the module across its long-range plan scenario set. Stability verdicts and the underlying variance decomposition are appended to the scenario-ranking chapter of the plan, with reversals reported by name.

Metropolitan planning organisation

Regional investment programme, Pacific Northwest

A metropolitan planning organisation applies the module to corridor investment rankings before programming. The stability report accompanies each ranking presented to the policy board.

National road administration

National corridor studies, Central Europe

A national road administration uses the module in corridor pre-feasibility studies. Rankings are reported with SP-41-conformant stability statements; non-transferable results are flagged for additional analysis.

Provincial road authority

Multi-year investment ranking, Canada

Two provincial road authorities run the module over multi-year project rankings, using the stability report to identify which adjacent projects are interchangeable at the margin.

Regional transport authority

Network strategy scenario testing, Australia

A regional transport authority applies the module to strategy scenarios spanning demand, network and pricing assumptions, and publishes stability statements alongside scenario results.

Motorway concessionaire

Capacity investment screening, Southern Europe

A motorway concessionaire uses the module to screen capacity investments under alternative traffic-growth scenarios before committing to detailed design.

The figures above are drawn from licence administration records as of 30 September 2026. Individual customer names are not published; enablement records are available to auditors under appropriate confidentiality arrangements.
