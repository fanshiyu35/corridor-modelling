Model Manager · Ranking Stability module now available

Planning software that says how firm a ranking really is.

Model Manager is our platform for regional travel-demand modelling. Its Project Evaluation family now includes the Ranking Stability (MTRS) module — a licensed implementation of the Multivariate Transport Ranking Stability test that reports, for any set of ranked scenarios, whether the ranking is stable, reversed, or not transferable.

Why stability reporting

Why stability reporting

Long-range plans rank scenarios; investment follows the ranking. The MTRS test answers the question a ranking alone cannot: whether the difference between first and second place is real.

Licensed method

The MTRS test is implemented under a software and technology licence from the University of California, Irvine (Agreement UCI-OTA-2025-001, 15 January 2025), following the procedure of NTRP Research Report No. 212 (May 2024).

Standard-conformant output

Module outputs conform to the reporting structure of SP-41, Reporting on the Stability of Scenario Rankings (September 2024).

Built for agencies

Stability verdicts are exported as standard report tables — STABLE / REVERSAL / NON-TRANSFERABLE — together with the underlying variance decomposition.
